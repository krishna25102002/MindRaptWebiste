MindRapt Technology — Corporate Website (eBusiness template)

MAIN FILES
  index.html       Homepage (all recommended sections)
  careers.html     Careers page

CUSTOM BRANDING
  assets/css/mindrapt.css   MindRapt-specific styles
  assets/css/main.css       Global accent colors updated for MindRapt

PREVIEW LOCALLY
  Open index.html in a browser, or from this folder run:
    npx --yes serve .
  Then open the URL shown (usually http://localhost:3000)

CUSTOMIZE BEFORE GO-LIVE
  - Add Bangalore office street address in index.html if needed (Chennai registered office is set)
  - Add your logo image in header (uncomment logo img in HTML if desired)
  - Point forms/contact.php and forms/newsletter.php to your mail backend
  - Add real social profile URLs in the footer
  - Replace stock photos under assets/img/ with your own brand imagery
