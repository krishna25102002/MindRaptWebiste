    Frequently Asked Questions

    Quick answers about how MindRapt works with customers, products, and custom development.

    1. Is MindRapt a services company or a product company?

    Both, by design. Our four pillars are our core identity; we ship named products (driver booking, fleet suite, HRMS) while also delivering custom web, mobile, cloud, and AI solutions for specific business needs.

    2. Can I partner on a product still marked “coming soon”?

    Yes. Early partners can influence roadmaps, pilot features, and regional rollouts. Contact us with your use case and timeline.

    3. Do you offer white-label or integrated deployments?

    We support branded deployments, API integrations with your existing systems, and modular components—especially in mobility, fleet, and enterprise automation.

    4. Which industries do you focus on today?

    Transportation and logistics, fleet operators, corporate HR and operations, and SMEs seeking digital transformation—with room to expand into ERP, fintech automation, and IoT as we grow.

    5. How do I start a business enquiry?

    Use the contact form below with a short description of your challenge. Our team will respond with next steps for discovery, demo, or proposal.
