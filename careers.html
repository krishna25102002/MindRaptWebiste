Work With Purpose

Product engineering across mobility, fleet intelligence, HRMS, and custom software

Why join MindRapt?

We are a product-oriented technology company. Our work spans on-demand mobility, fleet and logistics platforms, enterprise HRMS, and customer-driven custom builds—not throwaway demos or one-off scripts.

You will ship software that operators, drivers, and office teams use every day. We grow new products under one brand, so your work can scale with the company.

  • Ownership across mobile, web, cloud, and APIs—end to end where it matters.
  • Real-world domains: GPS, logistics, payments, and enterprise workflows.
  • Room to grow as we launch more products—without changing who we are as a company.
  • Teams based in Chennai and Bangalore, building for India and beyond.
